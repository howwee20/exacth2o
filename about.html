Our Team

The people behind
exactH2O

Bringing together plant biology, environmental sensing, and precision engineering to move irrigation out of the lab and into the real world.

Leadership

Built by scientists,
for scientists

exactH2O was founded at Michigan State University by a team with deep expertise spanning plant physiology, environmental sensing, and instrument engineering. Every design decision reflects years of firsthand frustration with imprecise irrigation in research settings — and the conviction that it could be done better.

Our Mission

Science-first
irrigation

exactH2O exists because imprecise watering was undermining good science. We saw researchers spending hours hand-watering pots, then struggling to justify their stress treatments to reviewers.

Our mission is to make publication-quality soil moisture control accessible at every scale of plant research and production — from a single bench to a multi-acre greenhouse complex.

01
Precision by Design
Every hardware and software decision is made with measurement accuracy as the primary constraint — not cost or convenience.
02
Open & Reproducible
Data is yours: open export formats, documented calibration, and no proprietary lock-in. Science requires transparency.
03
Built on Real Research
exactH2O was developed and validated in active MSU research greenhouses — not in a product lab disconnected from the scientific method.
04
Scalable & Supported
From first deployment to full greenhouse network, we provide technical support from people who understand both the biology and the engineering.
