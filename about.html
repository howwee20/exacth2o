Leadership

Built by scientists,
for scientists

exactH2O was founded at Michigan State University by a team with deep expertise spanning plant physiology, environmental sensing, and instrument engineering. Every design decision reflects years of firsthand frustration with imprecise irrigation in research settings — and the conviction that it could be done better.

01 —

Binod Basyal

Founder and Chief Executive Officer

Binod is a plant biologist with over a decade of experience studying plant–water relationships under drought stress. He holds a PhD in Biology and is a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Michigan State University, where his work focuses on soil moisture dynamics, plant physiology, and resilient lignocellulosic biofuel systems. Co-inventor of the exactH2O programmable drought and irrigation control system, he bridges science and practical application.
