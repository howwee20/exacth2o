How control works

One pot, your target

The controller waters a pot only when that pot's own measured moisture falls below the target you set for it. Move the target and watch one synthetic pot over three days.

measured moisturetargetvalve opening

Each time the pot's reading falls below the target, the controller opens its valve once and records it.

Synthetic illustration of the control rule, drawn with a simplified response model. It does not predict how your substrate, emitters or crop will respond, and nothing on this page connects to a controller.

Every experiment keeps its record

Target changes, calibrations and notes sit on the same timeline as the readings, so a step in a curve is explained by what happened, not guessed.
