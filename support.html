Common questions

How do I get access to the portal?+

Sign in with your invited email address. For missing or expired access, contact your project administrator or support with your project and account email.

How do I investigate an unexpected reading?+

Open the pot and choose Explain this reading to inspect its timestamp, raw signal, and calibration. Use Record to check recent changes. Check sensor placement before recalibrating. Values outside 0–100% VWC remain visible and flagged.

Where is exactH2O designed to be used?+

exactH2O supports plant research in greenhouses, bench systems, and container experiments. We confirm the sensor and irrigation setup with your quote.

How is a system sized for my greenhouse?+

System size depends on the number of benches, pots, treatment zones, sensors, and independently controlled irrigation lines. We review the facility and experimental requirements before recommending a configuration.

Can I review and export my data?+

The web portal provides live readings, historical views, irrigation history, and data export for analysis. We confirm any required formats or integrations while planning the system.

What happens if a sensor stops reporting?+

The portal flags missing or stale readings and identifies the affected pot. Support can help check the sensor and connection.

What is included with installation?+

Planning, on-site setup, and staff training are included. We confirm the schedule before delivery.
